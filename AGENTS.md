# Project-Specific AI Coding Rules & Context

## Verification & Rumor Monitoring Guidelines
- **Social Media Rumor Detection**: Always check for trending rumors, viral claims, voice memos, and media misinformation circulating across major social platforms, specifically **TikTok**, **Twitter (X)**, and **Facebook**, alongside local Nigerian news channels.
- **Evidence & Attribution**: Ensure rumor verification and fact-checking pipelines cite the originating social media platform (TikTok, Twitter/X, Facebook) and state verification status accurately (TRUE, FALSE, OUTDATED MEDIA, NEEDS MORE VERIFICATION).
- **Graceful Fallbacks**: When synthesizing live rumors or media forensics, maintain offline-resilient verified repositories and fast timeout guards to ensure instantaneous UI rendering.
